# Higgsfield AI Scam - Research & Documentation

A collection of research, transcripts, and evidence documenting alleged fraudulent practices, product misrepresentation, and ethical violations by Higgsfield AI.

## Contents

### 01 - Subscription Scam & Mass Bans
Transcript documenting the December 2024 incident where Higgsfield allegedly:
- Sold "unlimited" Black Friday plans, then mass-banned users starting December 19th
- Froze accounts globally (Europe, Asia, Americas) citing "suspicious payment activity"
- Replaced unlimited plans with a restrictive "battery" system demanding additional payments
- Engaged in predatory billing loops (one user charged five $39 invoices in a single day)
- Bought fake positive reviews on Trustpilot to suppress backlash
- A company moderator resigned and publicly accused the company of fraud

### 02 - Product Quality Expose
Transcript of a detailed technical review revealing:
- Camera tools (360 orbit, tilt, dolly, arc) consistently destroy subject faces and identities
- Free open-source alternatives (ComfyUI workflows, LoRAs) produce better results
- Higgsfield's best effects secretly use third-party models (Kling 2.5 Turbo, Minimax Hailuo 2.3) while marketing them as proprietary
- Users pay approximately 4.5x more than using the underlying models directly
- Only ~1 in 10 camera effects worked even remotely well
- "Soul" poster feature is just locked templates with mood filters

### 03 - Ethical Violations & Exploitation
Documentation of alleged ethical violations including:
- Non-consensual sexual deepfakes of real public figures
- Sexualized versions of children's media content
- A product feature literally named "Steal"
- Internal folders of deepfake content deemed too problematic to post publicly
- Rage-bait marketing strategy ("AI is destroying your jobs")
- Predatory addiction mechanics (manufactured urgency, FOMO tactics)
- Pattern of deleting evidence when caught
- Documented history of previous scam behavior
